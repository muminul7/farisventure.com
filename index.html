Track Record Before Faris Venture
Between 2023 and 2025, the founder completed 12 halal SME investments across 6 businesses, with an overall return of ~13.5%.
One lesson from that period shaped the rules here directly: the largest single ticket returned the weakest result. That is why financing is capped at BDT 7 lakh — where capital goes out and the business runs without us, exposure stays small. An equity partnership can go to BDT 10 lakh, because there we are inside the business, not watching from outside.

THE SHARIAH BASIS
An equity partnership here is Musharaka. Profit is shared at a ratio agreed in writing before any money moves. Loss is shared strictly in proportion to capital — that rule is not negotiable, because Shariah does not allow it to be.
HOW A PARTNERSHIP ENDS
This is agreed at the start, not improvised later. Two paths are possible: you buy back our share at its fair value on the day, or we stay on as a long-term partner taking a profit share. What is never done is fixing a buyback price in advance — that would make it a loan wearing a partnership's clothes.
HOW MANY WE TAKE
One or two partnerships a year. The mentorship is real time from one person, so it does not scale — and pretending otherwise would shortchange whoever is already in. The equity share itself is agreed per deal alongside the valuation; there is no fixed percentage.
