ABOUT US

Who We Are, What We Do

Faris Venture is a founder-backed halal investment venture that deploys capital into vetted Bangladeshi SMEs under Shariah-compliant structures. It currently operates as a single-investor fund. Every deal carries a defined agreement, a defined cap, and a clear expectation — nothing beyond that.
The goal is simple: prove that disciplined, Shariah-first capital can move faster and fairer than conventional interest-based financing for Bangladeshi entrepreneurs — without hiding the risk.
NOW — PHASE 1
Deploying the founder's own capital into a limited number of businesses, with hands-on vetting and a growing track record.
FUTURE VISION
Scaling this Phase 1 model into a broader halal SME financing platform, open to multiple investors.

Track Record Before Faris Venture
Between 2023 and 2025, the founder completed 10 halal SME investments across 6 businesses, with an overall return of ~13.5%.
One lesson from that period shaped Faris Venture's rules directly: the largest single ticket returned the weakest result. That's why no deal here exceeds BDT 7 lakh.
